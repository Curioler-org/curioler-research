# Curioler Research

Plain-language summaries of autism research, written for caregivers — not clinicians.

Each summary is sourced from peer-reviewed literature or clinical guidelines and rated by evidence tier so you know how much weight to give it.

**This site does not provide medical advice.** Always consult a qualified professional before making decisions about your child's care.
