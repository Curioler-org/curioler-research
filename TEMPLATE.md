# Curioler Summary Template

Use this as a reference when reviewing AI-generated drafts or writing summaries manually.

---

## Trust Tier Guide

| Tier | Label | What it means |
|------|-------|---------------|
| 1 | Systematic review / Meta-analysis | Synthesises many studies; highest confidence |
| 2 | Peer-reviewed study | Single study with methodology; good confidence |
| 3 | Clinical consensus / Guideline | Expert agreement or official guidance |
| 4 | Expert commentary / Opinion | Individual expert view; lowest confidence |
